Students briefly explore two of five Hugging Face demos, choose one,
identify its model, run three tests, and write an evidence-based review.
No passwords. No partner task. English/Spanish. Light/dark mode.

DOWNLOAD ONLY. No Google Sheets submission or automatic transmission.
Students download their response PDF/text and attach it to your class assignment.
They attach three saved evidence files separately. The response PDF does not
contain their screenshots, images, or audio—only the references they typed.

The external demos require internet access and may have queues or sign-in limits.
Students can switch demos if access prevents testing. Local browser autosave
does not replace downloading before leaving the device.
